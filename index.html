Whot

Nigerian Whot against the computer. You both start with 6 cards — play a card that matches the pile's shape or number, or play a Whot wild card and call any shape. Specials: 1 = Hold On (play again), 2 = opponent picks 2 and misses a turn, 5 = opponent picks 3 and misses a turn, 8 = Suspension (opponent misses a turn), 14 = General Market (both players pick 1). If you can't play, pick 1 from the market — if it plays, you may play it, otherwise the turn passes. First to empty their hand wins their stake plus 80% profit.

Help & FAQ

How TrybNetwork Trade & Save works in this simulation.

You have a Main Balance and a Mining balance. Deposits and withdrawals apply to Main Balance only, and Main is the only balance you can trade with. Mining profits collect in your Mining balance and only reach Main when you cash out — mining funds can never be staked on a trade.
Buy a mining pack from the Mining card — Starter ($5–$49, 3% per day), Pro ($50–$499, 7% per day), Industrial ($500–$4,999, 15% per day) or Whale ($5,000+, 30% per day) — and enter any amount in the pack's range; your daily profit is that percentage of your amount. Tap Start Mining and the rig runs for 24 hours, then stops and credits its profit to your Mining profits; you start each new cycle yourself. Profit cashout locks depend on the pack: Starter can cash out profits any day, Pro after 3 days, Industrial after 7 days and Whale after 30 days. Buying a miner is a straight purchase — the amount you put in is spent on the rig and isn't returned. Cashout moves your mining profits into Main Balance and never closes your miner.
Deposit and Withdraw on the Main Balance card create a request — the funds move only once the request is approved. For a deposit, enter the amount and choose Bank Transfer (OPay) or Crypto (USDT on TRC-20), send to the details shown, then tap I've sent it. Rejected requests leave your balance unchanged and are noted in your wallet history.
A 3 USDT welcome bonus is credited to your Main Balance when you start. It is held back from withdrawals until you have traded 10 USDT in total volume; after that it is fully yours to move. You also earn a 3 USDT referral bonus for every referral whose first deposit is 12 USDT — track it in the Refer & Earn panel and share your referral code from there.
Pick an asset (BTC, ETH, EURUSD, or gold), set a stake from your Main Balance, and bid Higher or Lower. Your stake is locked the moment you bid, and the bid sits at Awaiting result until the admin decides it. A win pays your stake back plus 90% profit on it into your Main Balance; a loss forfeits the locked stake. The chart streams live prices from TradingView for reference — results are decided by the admin, not by the chart.
With Martingale Mode on, your stake doubles after every loss and resets to your base stake after a win. It can recover small losses quickly, but a losing streak grows stakes exponentially and can wipe the balance — the arena shows a warning for a reason. Try it with virtual funds here before ever risking real money.
Open the menu at the top right and choose Payment Method to add a bank account or crypto wallet as a payout destination and mark one as default. This is a demo, so no real payouts are ever sent.
