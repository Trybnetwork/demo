Help & FAQ

How TrybNetwork Trade & Save works in this simulation.

You have a Main Balance and a Savings wallet, and they never mix. Deposits and withdrawals apply to Main Balance only, and Main is the only balance you can trade with. Savings has no deposit or withdrawal of its own — money enters and leaves Savings only by transferring to or from Main Balance, and savings funds can never be staked on a trade.
Savings earns 15% APY, accruing every second on whatever is saved. Every transfer into Savings becomes a separate lot that is locked for 7 days before it can move back to Main — each lot shows its own unlock countdown under your Savings balance. Interest you earn is never locked, and when you transfer out, your oldest unlocked lots are used first.
Deposit and Withdraw on the Main Balance card create a request — the funds move only once the request is approved. Rejected requests leave your balance unchanged and are noted in your wallet history.
A 3 USDT welcome bonus is credited to your Main Balance when you start. It is held back from withdrawals and transfers out until you have traded 10 USDT in total volume; after that it is fully yours to move. You also earn a 3 USDT referral bonus for every referral whose first deposit is 12 USDT — track it in the Refer & Earn panel and share your referral code from there.
Pick an asset (BTC, ETH, EURUSD, or gold), set a stake from your Main Balance, and bid Higher or Lower. Your stake is locked the moment you bid, and the bid sits at Awaiting result until the admin decides it. A win pays your stake back plus 85% profit into your Main Balance; a loss forfeits the locked stake. The chart streams live prices from TradingView for reference — results are decided by the admin, not by the chart.
With Martingale Mode on, your stake doubles after every loss and resets to your base stake after a win. It can recover small losses quickly, but a losing streak grows stakes exponentially and can wipe the balance — the arena shows a warning for a reason. Try it with virtual funds here before ever risking real money.
Open the menu at the top right and choose Payment Method to add a bank account or crypto wallet as a payout destination and mark one as default. This is a demo, so no real payouts are ever sent.
