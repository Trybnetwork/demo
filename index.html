Aviator

Every 15 seconds a new flight opens for bids — enter at least $1 and tap Place Bid before takeoff. The multiplier starts at 1.00x and climbs 0.10x every second, so 10 seconds of flight is 2.00x. Ayo

Ayo — the Nigerian mancala. You and the computer each own 6 pits starting with 4 seeds. Tap one of your pits to sow its seeds anti-clockwise, one per pit. If your last seed lands in a pit holding seeds, pick the whole pit up and keep sowing — your turn ends only when the last seed lands in an empty pit or on a 3 making 4. A pit that hits exactly 4 mid-sow is taken by its owner — land your last seed on a 3 to make 4 and you keep it. The round ends when 4 seeds remain on the board — most seeds grabbed wins 1.8× your stake.

Help & FAQ

How TrybNetwork works.

You have a Main Balance and a Mining balance. Deposits and withdrawals apply to Main Balance only, and Main is the only balance you can trade with. Mining profits collect in your Mining balance and only reach Main when you cash out — mining funds can never be staked on a trade.
Buy a miner from the Mining card — put in any amount from $5 to $50,000 and it earns 2% of it every 24 hours. Tap Start Mining and the rig runs for 24 hours, then stops and credits its profit to your Mining profits; you start each new cycle yourself. Profits unlock for cashout 7 days after purchase, then weekly after each cashout. Buying a miner is a straight purchase — the amount you put in is spent on the rig and isn't returned. Cashout moves your mining profits into Main Balance and never closes your miner.
Mining returns are generated from trading activity. Principal carries risk and returns are not guaranteed.
Deposit and Withdraw on the Main Balance card create a request — the funds move only once the request is approved. For a deposit, enter the amount and choose Bank Transfer (Palmpay) or Crypto (USDT on TRC-20), send to the details shown, then tap I've sent it. Rejected requests leave your balance unchanged and are noted in your wallet history.
A 3 USDT welcome bonus is credited to your Main Balance when you start. It is held back from withdrawals until you have traded 10 USDT in total volume; after that it is fully yours to move. You also earn a 3 USDT referral bonus for every referral whose first deposit is 12 USDT — track it in the Refer & Earn panel and share your referral code from there.
Pick an asset (BTC, ETH, EURUSD, or gold), set a stake from your Main Balance, and bid Higher or Lower. Your stake is locked the moment you bid, and the bid sits at Awaiting result until the admin decides it. A win pays your stake back plus 90% profit on it into your Main Balance; a loss forfeits the locked stake. The chart streams live prices from TradingView for reference.
With Martingale Mode on, your stake doubles after every loss and resets to your base stake after a win. It can recover small losses quickly, but a losing streak grows stakes exponentially and can wipe the balance.
Open the menu at the top right and choose Payment Method to save a bank account and/or a USDT wallet. At withdrawal time you choose which one gets paid — the details can't be edited during a withdrawal, only in Payment Method.
The withdrawal PIN is an optional 4-digit code you set in Payment Method. Once set, nobody can change your payout destinations without entering it — not even on your logged-in phone — and every withdrawal request needs the PIN too. If you forget it, tap Forgot PIN? and verify with your account password to set a new one.
